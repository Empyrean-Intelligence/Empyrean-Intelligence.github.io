Empyrean Intelligence

We are building superintelligence.

A mind that learns from its own experience, sets its own problems, checks its own answers, and keeps improving past the point where any human can follow. It may be the most important thing our species ever builds. It should be built for all of humanity.

What we have achieved

A model taught itself.

From nothing but its own attempts, checked exactly, it rose from 15 to 44 of 280 problems it had never seen. A second run did it again (20 to 37). The same training with a coin-flip reward fell to 0.
The ambitious version: a machine that keeps teaching itself past its own starting ceiling, with no human data.

The reward decides what it learns.

Same model, same training, only the reward changed. A correct checker taught it a new skill (5/100 to 15/100). A coin-flip reward broke it (GSM8K fell from 0.70 to 0.22; a second run on different tasks stayed near its start, 0.678).
The ambitious version: rewards a self-improving system cannot fool, the safety valve on every loop that improves itself.

We found where self-teaching breaks.

A learning loop fed problems and checks from outside climbed from 0.62 to 0.82. When the model set and graded its own, it fell to 0.27.
The ambitious version: the first loop that poses its own problems and does not collapse. That is the self-improvement loop, closed.
