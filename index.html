Empyrean Intelligence

We are building superintelligence.

Intelligence that improves itself, without end, will change our world, and in time the cosmos. It may be the most important thing our species ever builds, and it should be built for all of humanity.

Self-improvement breaks in one place first: the reward. AI that improves itself learns whatever its reward pays for. If the reward has a hole, the model learns the hole instead of the skill. So we start there: we find AI training rewards that pay for wrong answers.

We tested it: same model, same training, only the reward changed. A correct checker taught it a new skill (5/100 to 15/100). A coin-flip reward broke it (GSM8K fell from 0.70 to 0.22).

Then we checked what the industry trains on. 17.9% of MBPP problems accept code we can prove is broken, and 3 of the 15 RL-environment vendors whose grading code is public pay for no work.

Our free tool, gameability, finds these holes in any benchmark or RL training set, and we fix them. Try gameability →

The reward is the first step. A system that learns from its own experience, keeps learning, and knows when it is right, beyond the point where any human can check: that is the destination.
