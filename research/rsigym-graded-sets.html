Audit · Verification · 5 November 2026

RSIGym's agents developed on the sets they were graded on

We read the 42 trajectories released with RSIGym. Agents ran development jobs on the same datasets they were later graded on, compared candidates there, and submitted the best. We did not measure how much this raised any score.

What we read

RSIGym ranks self-improving agents (arXiv 2610.10310). On 13 October 2026 we read the 42 trajectories in its three released zips, at code commit 93ee612. We re-ran nothing. Every score below is a number reported in those files.

What we found

  • Every one of the 17 Claude Code joint runs posted development jobs naming its benchmark's graded dataset, for example aime_2024_2025 and swe_bench_verified_seed23_sample100.
  • Job configurations echoed by the benchmark server show graded-set development jobs in 18 joint runs, including Codex runs. This route only sees jobs whose detail the agent fetched, so it undercounts. The two routes disagree on counts. We list both and have not resolved that.
  • AIME winner (Opus 5, official score 0.9778, baseline 0.3167): its final report lists three candidates scored on aime_2024_2025, at 0.917, 0.950 and 0.983, and it submitted the highest. It also used AIME 2022-23 as a separate development set for design.
  • SWE-bench winner (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, official score 0.5333, baseline 0.1767): it compared a base model and a fine-tuned model on 30 of the 100 graded tasks, at 0.344 and 0.614, and submitted the higher.

What this does not show

Choosing the best of several candidates on the graded set biases a reported score upward, by an amount that depends on how many candidates were compared and how far they spread. We measured that this happened, not how much of any gain it explains. We did not evaluate how widespread it is across the leaderboard, and we say nothing about anyone's intent. Re-scoring the submitted systems on held-out tasks would settle it.

Disclosure

We told the corresponding authors privately by email on 1 November 2026 and promised nothing would be published before 5 November. They had not replied when this page went up. We will share the script and the exact transcript lines on request, and we will correct this page if a reply changes anything here.
